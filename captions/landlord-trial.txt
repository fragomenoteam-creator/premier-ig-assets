Buying a new home and keeping your old one as a rental? Fannie Mae now lets lenders use market rent from an appraisal, instead of a signed lease, to document that rental income. It applies to applications dated Nov. 1, 2026 or later.

A share of that rent can offset the old home's housing costs. If it falls short, the gap counts in your debt ratio. Rules vary by lender and loan program.
