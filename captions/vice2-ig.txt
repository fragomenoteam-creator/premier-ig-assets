There are no cheat codes for credit.

Vice City has cheat codes. A mortgage doesn't. Three credit myths, checked:

Myth: Checking your own credit lowers your score.
Fact: Looking at your own report or score is a soft check and doesn't hurt it.

Myth: Close your old cards before you apply.
Fact: Closing cards can lower your score. Ask before you close anything.

Myth: A quick trick fixes your credit before you apply.
Fact: Steady habits move it. A lender can walk you through a plan.
