Who pays closing costs? Sometimes the seller. 🏡

A seller credit is an amount the seller agrees to put toward the buyer's costs at closing. It can help with closing costs or a temporary buydown, and it's negotiated in the offer.

What a seller can contribute depends on the loan program and the property, so check before you write the offer.
