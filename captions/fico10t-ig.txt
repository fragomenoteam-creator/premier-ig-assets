FICO 10T has a date. 📊

FHFA set September 2028 as the target for full rollout of the FICO 10T credit score model at Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac. The end point is lender choice between FICO 10T and VantageScore 4.0. Dates after November 2026 are targets and can change.

Different score models can read the same file differently, so knowing where you stand now is worth it.
